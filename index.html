[SERVICES] THE RECORD

What the targets on the scope actually mean, in prose.

BRG 012°TRNG 9.6 NMTGT 001

Charter Captain

Crewed guest charters, day passages to a week in the islands. Corporate parties, family crew, anglers who take it seriously. I run the boat, the galley schedule and the weather calls so the owner can sit in the cockpit with the rest of them. Twenty-odd years of putting guests back on the dock in one piece and on time.

BRG 068°TRNG 6.4 NMTGT 002

Long & Short-Term Captaining

Standing assignment, relief for your captain's vacation, or a season at the helm. I keep the log, the systems and the coffee order. Boats I look after are ready to leave within two hours of a call. References from owners who kept me on for years are available; that is the whole sales pitch.

BRG 118°TRNG 4.4 NMTGT 003

Yacht Delivery & Repositioning

Coastal and blue water, voyage planning through arrival. Delivered 32 to 130 feet up and down the Pacific and through the Panama Canal. Weather windows, provisioning, a photo log, and a phone call when we tie up. The boat shows up somewhere other than the damage report.

BRG 162°TRNG 2.8 NMTGT 004

Yacht Management

Maintenance, systems, crew and stores oversight. I spent 23 years as a mechanic before I spent 30 as a captain, so a generator that sounds wrong gets fixed while it is still a $400 part and not a $40,000 rebuild. Preventive schedules, vendor control, and records you can actually read.

BRG 206°TRNG 5.2 NMTGT 005

Refit & Shipyard Management

Scopes, schedules, invoices, quality control. I sit on the owner's side of the table at the yard and catch the surprises while they are still cheap. Bottom jobs to full-systems rewires. If the invoice says teak, the deck gets teak.

BRG 252°TRNG 8.8 NMTGT 006

Owner & Crew Training

Handling, docking, navigation, radar and electronics, on your boat, in your marina, in weather that matters. From a weekend for a new owner to a week for a deck crew. Insurance sign-off provided when the person at the wheel can credibly handle the boat, and not before.

BRG 296°TRNG 10.8 NMTGT 007

Pre-Purchase Inspection, Survey Sea Trials & Insurance Sign-offs

Buyers and brokers: I run the trials that tell you what the boat is. Load tests, alignments, top-end speed, maneuvering checks, electronics exercised. Then a report in plain English, and a straight answer to the only question that matters: would I put my own family on it?

BRG 338°TRNG 11.6 NMTGT 008

Yacht Repossession Moves

Licensed captain for lenders and recovery agents. Quiet, legal, insured. Vessels moved and secured, inventories documented, engines run before they are parked. This work rewards discretion and paperwork. I bring both.

[TRANSMISSIONS RECEIVED] QUESTIONS AS FOLLOWS

“What areas do you cover?”

Home ports are Newport Beach, San Diego, Marina del Rey and Long Beach. That means Southern California day charters and lessons, deliveries up and down the West Coast, BC to Alaska, the Gulf of Mexico via the Panama Canal, and the East Coast from Florida to the Carolinas.

“Can you take my boat offshore for a delivery?”

Yes, within reason. I hold a USCG Master of Steam or Motor Vessels, 500 GT / 200 GRT, International (credential #2776127). I have delivered boats from 32 to 130 feet and taken the wheel on motor yachts up to 80 meters. If I am outside my ticket or my depth, I will tell you before we cast off, not after.

“How do you charge?”

Day rate for charters, lessons and sea trials; weekly or monthly for standing and relief assignments; a flat voyage quote for deliveries with estimated transit time spelled out first. Refit and management work is scoped in writing. Nobody enjoys talking money, so I keep it short and put it in writing.

“Do you sign off for insurance?”

I provide owner and captain sign-off after training on your boat, and I run pre-purchase sea trials for buyers and brokers. I sign when the person at the wheel can actually handle the boat in the conditions they will face. If the answer is no, you will know why.

“Can I meet you before hiring me?”

Yes. Come down to the dock, walk the boat, and see the logbook. References from long-term owners, brokers and recovery agents available on request. The crew list is short: it is mostly me.

“Do you bring a crew?”

On deliveries I can work solo or with deckhand support, and I can help you hire relief crew you can trust. On refits I run the project and work with your yard's people. On charters, tell me the guest count and we plan the service accordingly.
